# Analysis Methodology

This project follows a structured Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) workflow.

---

# Step 1 — Database Exploration

Understand the overall database structure.

Activities:

- Identify schemas
- Review tables
- Examine columns
- Count records

---

# Step 2 — Dimension Exploration

Explore descriptive business entities.

Includes:

- Customers
- Products

Objectives:

- Understand categories
- Explore customer demographics
- Analyze product hierarchy

---

# Step 3 — Date Exploration

Analyze historical data.

Includes:

- First Order Date
- Last Order Date
- Monthly Trends
- Yearly Trends

---

# Step 4 — Measure Exploration

Generate business KPIs.

Examples:

- Total Sales
- Total Orders
- Average Sales
- Average Order Value
- Average Quantity

---

# Step 5 — Magnitude Analysis

Measure performance across business dimensions.

Examples:

- Sales by Country
- Sales by Category
- Sales by Product Line

---

# Step 6 — Ranking Analysis

Identify best and worst performers.

Examples:

- Top Customers
- Top Products
- Bottom Products
- Highest Revenue Categories

---

# Step 7 — Business Questions

Answer analytical business questions using SQL.

The output provides actionable insights for business decision-making.
